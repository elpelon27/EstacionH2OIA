## Hechos clave (Qwen local)

- **Fact:** Mycoplasma bovis is a pathogen causing pneumonia, arthritis, and mastitis in cattle worldwide.
- **Fact:** The prevalence of M. bovis antibodies in bulk tank milk (BTM) increased from 17% to 28% when including primiparous (PP) cows.
- **Fact:** Larger herds are associated with higher antibody levels in PP cows, indicating a risk factor for the infection.
- **Fact:** The introduction of cattle is also associated with higher antibody levels in PP cows, suggesting it influences the level of M. bovis antibodies.
- **Fact:** Antibody testing can help identify infected herds early and control further spread of the infection.
- **Fact:** Seropositive bulk tank milk (BTM) indicates a herd-level prevalence of M. bovis infection based on antibodies.
- **Fact:** The presence of M. bovis in PP cows is associated with increased antibiotic use, highlighting the economic impact.
- **Fact:** There are no effective vaccines against M. bovis as of now, emphasizing the need for control and prevention strategies.
- **Fact:** Serological methods like ELISA can be used to detect asymptomatic carriers of M. bovis.
- **Fact:** The prevalence of M. bovis in Swedish dairy herds was 4.8% in a national screening conducted in 2019, with regional variations ranging from 0 to 20%.
- **Fact:** Herd-level screening using antibodies in BTM or milk from individual animals can be used to identify exposed herds.
- **Fact:** The duration of M. bovis antibody presence is not well understood, as some cows remained seropositive for at least 1.5 years after the index case.
- **Fact:** Antibody testing has been found useful in monitoring herd infection status for other infections like RSV and coronavirus.
- **Technique:** Mixed linear regression models were used to identify herd characteristics associated with positive antibody tests in PP cows.
- **Technique:** The use of ID Screen Mycoplasma bovis Indirect ELISA (IDvet) showed high sensitivity (92.5%–94%) and specificity (92%–99.3%) for detecting M. bovis antibodies.
- **Technique:** Studies have used ELISA to detect antibodies in dairy herds, providing a useful tool for identifying exposed herds.
- **Technique:** The use of primiparous cows as a sampling group has been found useful in monitoring herd infection status for other infections like RSV and coronavirus.
- **Recommendation:** Repeated sampling of a few PP cows can help find newly infected herds early, motivating the need for such practices.
