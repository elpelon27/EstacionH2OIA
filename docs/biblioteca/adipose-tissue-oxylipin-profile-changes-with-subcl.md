## Hechos clave (Qwen local)

- **Fact 1:** During the periparturient period, lipolysis of adipose tissue occurs naturally in dairy cows. However, when rates exceed other tissues' capacity to use nonesterified fatty acids (NEFA), it can lead to ketosis and related diseases.
- **Fact 2:** Polyunsaturated fatty acids (PUFA) can be oxidized into oxylipins, which modulate inflammation and metabolism in dairy cows. The objective of this study was to identify depot-specific differences on adipose tissue oxylipin profile in dairy cows with and without subclinical ketosis.
- **Fact 3:** Subcutaneous adipose tissue (SAT) from the flank and visceral adipose tissue (VAT) from the omentum were collected through laparotomy from multiparous dairy cows aged between 5 to 14 days in milk (DIM).
- **Fact 4:** The study divided the animals into two groups based on blood beta-hydroxybutyrate (BHB) levels: nonketotic (NK; BHB ≤0.8 mmol/L) and subclinical ketotic (SCK; BHB >1.4 and ≤2.6 mmol/L).
- **Fact 5:** A targeted lipidome capable of detecting 154 oxylipins was performed in paired SAT and VAT samples from all animals.
- **Fact 6:** The study found that subclinical ketosis (SCK) was associated with a decreased abundance of thromboxane-B2 (TXB2), an increased abundance of prostaglandin-A2 (PGA2), and an increase in prostaglandin-E2 (PGE2).
- **Fact 7:** In periparturient cows, increased infiltration of macrophages was observed in omental VAT compared to tailhead SAT. This difference was particularly pronounced in cows with displaced abomasum.
- **Fact 8:** The study evaluated the depot-specific effects of identified oxylipins on dairy cow adipocyte function in vitro.
- **Fact 9:** Flunixin meglumine, a cyclooxygenase inhibitor, was administered post-surgery to reduce pain and inflammation. Animals were clinically monitored for three days after surgery before skin sutures were removed.
- **Fact 10:** SAT and VAT samples were snap frozen immediately after collection and stored in liquid nitrogen until lipidome analysis.
