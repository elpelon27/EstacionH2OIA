## Hechos clave (Qwen local)

- **Fact:** Enzootic bovine leukosis (leukosis) is a chronic, contagious infectious disease caused by Bovine Leukemia Virus (BLV), prevalent in North American dairy herds.
- **Fact:** The prevalence of leukosis varies significantly among Canadian dairy herds; on average, 39% of cows tested were positive for leukosis within herds testing positive.
- **Fact:** Genetic selection could contribute to permanent improvement in dairy cattle resistance to leukosis.
- **Fact:** A total of 117,349 milk ELISA test records from 96,779 Holstein cows across 950 Canadian herds between 2007 and 2021 were provided by Lactanet Canada.
- **Fact:** Heritabilities for leukosis resistance using a linear animal model and BLUP or single-step GBLUP methodology were estimated at 0.10 (SE = 0.001) and 0.07 (SE < 0.001), respectively.
- **Fact:** Genetic evaluation for leukosis resistance is possible in Canadian Holstein cattle, with potential to be included in national selection indices.
- **Fact:** The study aimed to assess the prevalence and impact of leukosis in Canada and explore genetic architecture of leukosis resistance using pedigree and genomic information.
- **Fact:** Candidate genes for leukosis resistance were identified on chromosome 23, including some associated with the major histocompatibility complex (MHC).
- **Fact:** Genetic evaluation for leukosis resistance is feasible and could be considered for inclusion in Canadian national selection indices.
- **Fact:** The low PVL phenotype of BLV infection is considered relatively safe as it reduces the risk of spreading Bovine Leukemia Virus (BLV).
- **Fact:** Genetic architecture studies on leukosis resistance are sparse, with research focusing more on polymorphisms affecting proviral load (PVL) and genetic associations with susceptibility or resilience.
- **Fact:** The study used a linear animal model and BLUP methodology to estimate heritabilities for leukosis resistance in Canadian Holstein cattle.
- **Fact:** Genetic evaluation of leukosis resistance is possible, indicating that it can be considered for inclusion in national selection indices in Canada.
- **Fact:** Genetic parameters were estimated using genomic information, including GEBV (Genomic Estimated Breeding Values) predictions.
- **Fact:** A genome-wide association study (GWAS) was performed to better understand the genetic background of leukosis in Canadian Holstein cattle.
