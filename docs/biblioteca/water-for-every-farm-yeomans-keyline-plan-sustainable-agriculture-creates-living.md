- **Farm Dams Construction Basics**: Methods for constructing farm dams using double vibration construction method, ensuring proper site preparation, dam wall construction, maintenance, and finishing stages.
- **Farming Roads**: Guidelines on locating farming roads along boundary fences, watershed lines, or channels to facilitate efficient irrigation systems.
- **Trees in Keyline Scale of Permanence**: Strategies for planting trees within the keyline scale, including contour strip forests, fire prevention measures, and soil improvement through urban effluent disposal.
- **Irrigation Channels**: Techniques for constructing both diversion and flood-flow irrigation channels, emphasizing proper channel grading, construction methods, and water gate installation.
- **Fencing in Keyline**: Methods for normalizing and formalizing fencing to ensure efficient property planning and maintenance of keyline systems.

- **Soil Fertility**: Discussion on biological soil fertility accelerated through keyline methods, practical experiences in different regions, and succession of climaxes created by depth of cultivation.
- **Trace Elements**: Insights into the availability of primary minerals and common misunderstandings about plant nutrition related to keyline principles.
- **Rothamsted Revisited**: Analysis of Rothamsted research revisited within the context of keyline practices, highlighting its relevance in modern agricultural management.
- **Water Harvesting as Part of Keyline**: Strategies for water harvesting and storage, such as irrigation dams filled with 15 mm (60 points) of rain, showcasing their effectiveness in managing droughts and enhancing land conservation.
