## Hechos clave (Qwen local)

- **Mastitis is a significant economic issue affecting dairy farmers worldwide, with costs estimated at $22 billion annually (Rasmussen et al., 2024).**
- **Staphylococcus aureus is one of the most frequently isolated bacteria associated with mastitis in cattle (Sharifi et al., 2023), causing subclinical and chronic mastitis due to its resistance to antibiotics and immune evasion capabilities (Lahouassa et al., 2007).**
- **Cyclophilin A (CyPA) is a cytosolic protein known for binding cyclosporine, with recent studies showing it has chemotactic activity in recruiting inflammatory cells (Takanashi et al., 2015; Liao et al., 2021).**
- **In bovine mastitis, CyPA is detected in milk and secreted abundantly at the onset of the disease (Takanashi et al., 2015), correlating with SCC levels.**
- **CyPA secretion correlates significantly with SCC concentrations in milk, suggesting its potential as a marker for mastitis onset (Takanashi et al., 2015).**
- **Intramammary infusion of Staphylococcus aureus into cattle led to increased expression and secretion of CyPA on mammary epithelia and into milk (Takanashi et al., 2015), indicating its role in immune cell migration during mastitis.**
- **CyPA is secreted extracellularly during infection, hypoxia, and oxidative stress (Sherry et al., 1992; Yurchenko et al., 2010).**
- **Bovine CyPA not only has chemotactic activity but also enhances the expression of inflammatory cytokines in mammary epithelial cells upon stimulation (Islam et al., 2020).**
- **The SCC is a commonly used marker for evaluating milk quality and udder health, with elevated levels correlating with intramammary infection (Lahouassa et al., 2007; Schukken et al., 2003).**
- **Neutrophils account for more than 90% of SCC in milk at the onset of mastitis (Lahouassa et al., 2007), highlighting their importance in inflammatory responses.**
- **CyPA is present in mammary epithelial cells and detected in alveoli and ducts during mastitic conditions (Takanashi et al., 2015).**
- **The development of mastitis remains a significant challenge in the dairy industry, warranting further investigation into its inflammatory mechanisms (Ferchiou et al., 2023).**
- **CyPA secretion correlates with S. aureus infection and can be used as an indicator for monitoring mastitis progression (Takanashi et al., 2015; Islam et al., 2020).**
