## Hechos clave (Qwen local)

- Wild nopal varieties are often more suitable than cultivated ones for marginal land conditions due to their greater resilience and ability to withstand harsher growing conditions.
- The nutritional value of wild nopal can vary, but is generally comparable to that of cultivated varieties.
- Forage quality needs to be considered carefully when using nopal as a feed source for livestock.
