## Hechos clave (Qwen local)

- **Animal Products Notice: Colombia OMAR amendment 3** comes into force on the date of signing.
- **Amendment 3** was issued in December 2017, replacing **Colombia OMAR Amendment 2** from November 2017.
- The notice is issued under section 60 of the Animal Products Act 1999 and specifies requirements for animal material and products intended for export to Colombia.
- **Ministry for Primary Industries (MPI)** is responsible for issuing this notice, with **Ivan Veljkovic** acting as Acting Manager Market Access.
- The document includes specific requirements for dairy products, fish and fish products, trade samples, and other categories of animal products.
- **ICA** (Colombian Agricultural Institute) is responsible for animal and plant health and biosecurity in Colombia. **INVIMA** (Colombian Institute of Medicines and Food Surveillance) oversees food safety.
- **Exporters must ensure compliance with product registration requirements through INVIMA, which includes registering consumer-ready products for human consumption and ingredients used in other food products.
- **Establishment Registration** is required from ICA, but the process may be handled by INVIMA in the future pending consultation with Colombia's trading partners.
- **Official Assurances** are provided by way of export certificates issued under section 62 of the Animal Products Act 1999.
- **Particular attention should be paid to product and establishment registration responsibilities**, as clarified in this amendment.
- Exporters must notify MPI if animal products are refused entry by a foreign government, with specific procedures available on the MPI website for non-dairy or dairy products.
- The notice applies to areas regulated by the Ministry for Primary Industries and may include generic requirements applicable to multiple markets.
